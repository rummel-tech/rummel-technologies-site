Our Story

Built for people who take their life seriously

Rummel Technologies was founded on a simple belief: the best personal software should work together, not in silos. We've spent too long jumping between disconnected apps that each tell part of the story but never the whole one.

Every app we build is designed to stand alone as a polished, focused tool. But they're also designed to connect — sharing context and intelligence through Artemis so that your fitness, nutrition, finances, home, and work can finally inform each other the way they do in real life.

We're a small, focused team committed to building software that respects your attention, protects your data, and genuinely improves your daily life.
